Encanto by Paloma

A hidden rainforest valley you can walk through, right in your browser. No download, no sign-up, just step in and listen.

About the experience

Encanto is an interactive 3D rainforest built entirely in code. Every giant tree, orchid, macaw and drop of rain is generated as you arrive, so the valley is made fresh for each visit. It's a place to slow down, wander the red clay trail and let the sound of the forest do the rest.

Your journey

You've gathered 0 of 7 spirit seeds. Follow the trail and look for the glowing green lights.

  • Mouse or ← → look and turn
  • WASD or ↑ ↓ move
  • Shift sprint · Space jump · Esc pause
  • Left thumb: walk
  • Right thumb: drag to look
  • Run toggles sprint · Jump to hop

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to enjoy the valley.

  • Keyboard: you can explore with the keyboard alone, and every menu works with Tab, Enter and Esc.
  • Sound: forest audio can be switched off at any time with the Sound button.
  • Motion: if your device is set to reduce motion, we turn off fades and transitions.
  • This page: all site information lives here as plain text, so it works with screen readers.
  • Feedback: found something hard to use? Tell us on GitHub (opens in a new tab).
